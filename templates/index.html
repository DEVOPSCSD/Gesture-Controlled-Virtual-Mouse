How to use

  • Move: Raise only your index finger.
  • Left click: Pinch thumb and index while keeping the other fingers raised.
  • Right click: Raise index and middle fingers only.
  • Drag: Make a closed fist, then move your hand.
  • Stop: Click STOP to release the mouse and close the camera.

Allow camera access when your browser asks. MediaPipe needs an internet connection to load its model files.
